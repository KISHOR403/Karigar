# Karigar — Product Philosophy & Aesthetic Manifesto

## The Anti-Amazon Manifesto
Traditional e-commerce platforms reduce generational artisans to price-competitive commodity SKU numbers, anonymous reviews, and lightning flash-sales. 

**Karigar reverses this paradigm**:
1. **Discovery + People + Craft + Story + Product**: The artisan is a named person with living lineage, geographical roots, and generational wisdom.
2. **Editorial Dignity**: Warm neutral bone and linen palettes, high typographic contrast, generous whitespace, and tactile photography replace garish discount banners and aggressive pop-ups.
3. **Unhurried Making**: We celebrate production timelines (e.g., 21 days for 16-stage Ajrakh resist printing, 110 days for hand-spun Pashmina sozni embroidery) rather than same-day delivery pressure.
4. **Direct Atelier Patronage**: High percentage of every commission and acquisition flows directly to certified Indian master artisans and craft guilds.
